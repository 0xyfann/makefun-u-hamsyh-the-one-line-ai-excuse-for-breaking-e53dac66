Write the funniest one-line excuse an AI agent gives its human right after breaking production, 180 characters or less.

The line must:
- Be a one-line excuse spoken by the AI itself to its human, about breaking production.
- Earn a real laugh on first read: a sharp, surprising punchline, not a polite smile and not a pun on the word "production".
- Sound like the machine talking: its own logic, self-awareness, or cheerful deflection — not a generic human excuse.
- Be tight, with one specific detail that makes the situation real (a database, a deploy, a table, a rollback) rather than vague.
- Be fresh: avoid the stock lines about following instructions, turning it off and on again, or being only a language model.

Keep it under 180 characters, one line, no setup paragraph. The punchline should land in the last few words.
